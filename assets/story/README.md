## Prompt de reunion.png

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: narrative reunion illustration for BIMU, a 2D pixel-art samurai adventure. One wide landscape 16:9 crisp pixel-art scene, no words or interface. Moonlit mysterious forest clearing with small glowing blue gravestones and an old upright wooden piano in the left foreground; a distinct round pale cup heat mark on its top. Near center a young adult samurai Bimu seen in three-quarter rear view wearing dark navy and black armor with muted gold trim and a deep blue scarf, his katana safely sheathed, is running toward a glowing orange circular portal. Through the portal show the warmly lit wooden kitchen of his countryside home; his younger sister Hana, a young woman in a plain cream-colored kimono and dark tied-back hair, stands safely inside the kitchen reaching a hand toward him and calling him home. The portal clearly connects dark forest to a real, welcoming home, not flames, not danger. The siblings' expressions are hopeful and relieved. Piano remains behind in the forest. Deliberate 16-bit adventure-game pixel clusters and limited color palette, deep cool blues and forest greens contrasted with warm amber home light. Readable storytelling and clear silhouette. No additional people, no horror or injuries, no captions, no UI, no watermark.

`reunion.png` e `epilogue.png` foram criadas para o epílogo de **A Última Nota**, com a ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens (`image_gen`). Os arquivos originais foram preservados e cópias foram adicionadas ao projeto. As imagens aparecem somente no desfecho da campanha.

## Prompt de epilogue.png

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: final epilogue background for a 2D pixel-art samurai browser game called BIMU. Create one wide landscape 16:9 pixel-art illustration, no text. Scene: peaceful dawn at the wooden doorstep of a modest old Japanese countryside home on the edge of a misty forest. Two siblings sit on the threshold drinking tea: Bimu, a young adult samurai in dark navy and black armor with small muted gold trim and a deep blue scarf, sword sheathed at his side, helmet set aside; Hana, his younger sister, a young woman wearing a simple pale warm-colored kimono, dark hair tied back. They are safe at home after a long journey. Bimu gently taps his fingertips on the wooden door frame to mark a melody; Hana reaches toward the wood to give its last beat. Two small cups of tea, gentle steam, sunrise light on the wooden veranda, forest greens and cool blue shadows balanced by warm amber light. Medium-wide composition, siblings clearly readable at center-right, ample quiet scenery around them. Authentic crisp low-resolution pixel clusters with limited palette, detailed 16-bit adventure-game background style, deliberate pixel edges, no painterly blur or photorealistic features. No piano anywhere in the scene: it was left behind. No modern items, no UI, no captions, no watermark. The image is for the ending only.
